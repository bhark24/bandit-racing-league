Weekly Truck Fleet Management

Each team must purchase and maintain a dedicated Craftsman Truck for each of their rostered drivers (both full-time and part-time). Deductions from race wear, crashes, or engine failures will lower condition ratings, requiring franchise capital for repairs.

🔧 Repair Costs & Options • Manual Repairs: Restores chassis at $500 per 1% condition.
• Bulk Repairs: Check multiple trucks to repair all of them in a single transaction, requiring password entry only **once**.
• Loan Lock: Manual repairs are disabled if the team has an active emergency loan.
💥 Race Incidents (Auto-Applied) • Engine Blown DNF: Flat $45,000 replacement (resets to 100%).
• Fast Repair (Finished): Flat $83,250 fee (resets to 100%).
• Fast Repair (DNF/Totaled): Replaced with new truck for $185,000 (resets to 100%).
