General League, Website, and Broadcast Likeness Release

BANDIT RACING LEAGUE • OFFICIAL PARTICIPATION RELEASE

1. Purpose & Scope of Release

To participate in the BANDIT RACING LEAGUE, the Driver (Licensor) hereby grants to the BANDIT RACING LEAGUE (Licensee) and its partners the right to capture, display, broadcast, and promote their competitive participation. This release governs the usage of the Driver's name, car number, paint scheme designs, driver statistics, and racing likeness across the league's public media channels.

2. Authorized Media Channels & Platforms
  1. League Website (bandit-racing-league.net): Licensee is authorized to display the Driver's name, car number, custom paint schemes, team affiliation, driver profile, race statistics, lap times, and telemetry data on the official league website.
  2. Official Broadcasts (SimTrax Broadcasting): Licensee is authorized to broadcast the Driver's live racing footage, custom car designs, in-game voice communications, team radio, and name/driver tags on all streaming and video platforms utilized by SimTrax Broadcasting (official league broadcasting partner), including but not limited to YouTube and Twitch.
  3. League Social Media: Licensee is authorized to feature the Driver's name, driver number, screenshots, video replays, race highlights, and quotes across all social media outlets managed by the league (including Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, and X).
3. Royalty-Free League Participation

The Driver acknowledges that this release is granted voluntarily as a condition of registration and participation in the BANDIT RACING LEAGUE. The Driver agrees that this likeness release is fully royalty-free, and they shall receive no monetary compensation, fees, or payouts for broadcasts, website listings, or social media promotion.

ℹ️ SEPARATE MERCHANDISE AGREEMENT OPT-IN:
This release does NOT cover the commercial manufacturing or sale of driver-specific merchandise. Any commercial merchandise creation and associated profit-sharing (e.g., custom apparel sales on Geezer Authentic's) must be governed under a separate, driver-specific Merchandising Agreement.

4. Term & Termination
  1. This release remains active for the duration of the Driver's participation in any BRL-sanctioned season.
  2. Driver may revoke future promotional usage by providing thirty (30) days written notice of league withdrawal. Upon withdrawal, future broadcasts and new social media posts will cease, though archived broadcast recordings and historical race logs on the website will remain intact.
