The official app for the BANDIT RACING LEAGUE.

Designed by Sim Racers, Built for Absolute Focus.

Whether you are hitting the apex or managing a high-stakes pit strategy, information is speed. The Geezer Telemetry Dashboard is a premium, lightweight companion app built specifically for iRacing drivers who demand real-time, zero-latency data without screen clutter. Engineered to run seamlessly on a secondary monitor or dedicated telemetry screen, the dashboard bridges the gap between your car’s telemetry stream and your race strategy, giving you the edge you need to stay ahead of the pack.

App Setup Instructions

1. Verify & Download

Unlock the portal above using your iRacing Customer ID and download the installer (`Geezer_Dashboard_Setup.exe`).

2. Install & Auto-Start

Run the setup. The app extracts the files, configures the desktop shortcut icon, and launches the dashboard automatically.

3. Sync & Race

Start your iRacing session. The dashboard automatically syncs and starts displaying live telemetry and audio warnings.
