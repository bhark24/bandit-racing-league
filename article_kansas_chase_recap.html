Season 16 Chase Round 2 • The Wendy Jo Memorial 110 at Kansas Speedway • Official Race Report

Last-Lap Masterpiece at Kansas: Nick Nickerson Storms From 19th to Pass Benjamin Lacy on Lap 117 in Double-Overtime Thriller!

After Joshua L. Adams (#22) sets a blistering early pace with a 5-second lead and sweeps Stages 1 & 2 (66 laps led, race-high 54 points), a caution-filled final stage forces two Green-White-Checkered attempts—setting up Nick Nickerson's (#2) last-lap pass on Benjamin Lacy (#7) to lead a 1-2-3 GFR Racing sweep!

Watch the full SimTrax Broadcast replay of The Wendy Jo Memorial 110 (sponsored by Wes Fuller #35 in loving memory of Wendy Jo Jackson) at Kansas Speedway.

KANSAS CITY, KS — Round 2 of the Season 16 Bandit Racing League Chase for the Championship—The Wendy Jo Memorial 110 at Kansas Speedway, sponsored by Wes Fuller (#35) in loving memory of his mother Wendy Jo Jackson and presented by JoKa Creations 3D Printing—delivered a classic tale of two halves under the lights in the Heartland.

The early part of the race featured smooth, high-speed green-flag rhythm where 937 Racing star Joshua L. Adams (#22) put on an absolute clinic, rocketing out to a 5-second lead early and sweeping both Stage 1 and Stage 2. But when the final stage erupted with a flurry of cautions—forcing two Green-White-Checkered (GWC) overtime attempts and extending the scheduled 110-lap race to 117 laps—the night came down to a one-lap showdown between GFR Racing teammates Nick Nickerson (#2) and points leader Benjamin I. Lacy (#7). Starting all the way back in 19th on the grid, Nickerson pulled off a masterpiece of a pass on the final lap to beat Lacy to the stripe by 0.243 seconds, with Kevin Foster (#8) crossing 3rd (-0.356s) to complete a clean 1-2-3 GFR Racing podium sweep!

Stages 1 & 2: Smooth Early Rhythm & Joshua Adams' 5-Second Lead

Qualifying set the tone for a razor-close night at the 1.5-mile Kansas tri-oval, as ESR’s Ethan Sikorski (#24) captured the Pole Award (`30.6028s`) by just `0.0037` seconds over 937 Racing’s Joshua L. Adams (#22, `30.6065s`), with race sponsor Wes Fuller (#35 | Title Town Racing) qualifying a stellar 3rd (`30.6197s`) in his own memorial tribute event.

Once the green flag waved, Adams wasted zero time taking command. Powering the #22 Toyota Tundra around the progressive high banking on Lap 1, Adams set a blistering early pace during a smooth opening stretch—stretching his advantage out to a massive 5-second lead over the field! With only two brief cautions (for 3 total laps) interrupting Stage 1, Adams led all 33 laps of the opening stage to claim the Stage 1 victory (+10 stage points) ahead of Ethan Sikorski (#24, P2), Joey Cruz (#28, P3), Scott Sanderson (#93, P4), Kevin Foster (#8, P5), Jackson Knaak (#00, P6), and Benjamin Lacy (#7, P7).

Stage 2 followed a similar script. After brief pit-cycle lead exchanges where Kevin Foster (#8) led 1 lap and Benjamin Lacy (#7) led 4 laps, Adams marched right back to the front and led 28 of the 33 laps in Stage 2 to complete a Stage 1 & Stage 2 sweep (+20 stage points) ahead of Peter Murphy (#38, P2), Jackson Knaak (#00, P3), Tre Ellis (#63, P4), Benjamin Lacy (#7, P5), and Nick Nickerson (#2, P6)—who had already quietly climbed from 19th on the starting grid into the top six.

⚡ JOSHUA L. ADAMS (#22) — MAXIMIZING STAGE & BONUS POINTS

Even though late-race restart traffic shuffled the #22 back to a 9th-place finish on Lap 117, Joshua Adams walked away with a race-high 54 total points (28 finish pts + 20 stage pts + 4 Most Laps Led bonus + 2 Clean Race 0x bonus)—vaulting him past Scott Sanderson into 2nd place in the Championship Chase (2,156 pts, just 14 points behind Benjamin Lacy)!

Stage 3: Caution Chaos, Two GWC Attempts & Nickerson's Last-Lap Masterpiece

After 66 laps of relatively clean, rhythmic intermediate racing, the complexion of the race changed dramatically in the final stage. While Benjamin Lacy (#7) rotated to the front and paced the field for 34 laps looking poised for his 5th victory of Season 16, three separate Stage 3 cautions (consuming 13 yellow-flag laps) repeatedly stacked the pack back up nose-to-tail and wiped out every multi-truck breakaway.

With the scheduled distance of 110 laps expiring under yellow, the field lined up for two high-intensity Green-White-Checkered (GWC) overtime attempts. On the final overtime restart, GFR Racing teammates Benjamin Lacy (#7) and Nick Nickerson (#2) broke clear at the front of the pack with Kevin Foster (#8) and Dylan McDonald (#9) right in their tire tracks. Entering the final lap (Lap 117), Nickerson set up Lacy with surgical precision—executing a masterpiece of a pass on the last lap to seize the lead and rocket across the finish line by 0.243 seconds!

Lacy crossed 2nd (-0.243s, 34 laps led, 0x incidents, 51 points), while GFR Racing owner Kevin Foster (#8) took 3rd (-0.356s, 1 lap led, 0x incidents, 46 points) in his 198th career SimRacerHub start—giving GFR Racing an unprecedented 1-2-3 podium sweep where all three drivers recorded 0x incident points!

Behind the GFR trio, Legacy Racing’s Dylan McDonald (#9) charged to 4th (-0.533s, 35 points), while defending Race 11 Kansas winner Michael Rakes (#47 | 937 Racing) rallied from 18th on the grid (+13 spots) to finish 5th (-1.035s, 32 points)—maintaining his remarkable streak as the only driver in the league with Top-10 finishes in all six 1.5-mile intermediate races this season! Scott Sanderson (#93) finished 6th (38 points), pole-sitter Ethan Sikorski (#24) took 7th (40 points), race sponsor Wes Fuller (#35) earned a heartfelt 8th-place finish in his mother's memorial race, Joshua Adams (#22) finished 9th (54 points), and Tre Ellis (#63) climbed 11 spots from 21st to round out the Top 10.
