New Week. New Track. High Stakes in the Desert!

The Bandit Racing League Craftsman Truck Series rolls into the desert tonight for 140 grueling laps under the lights at Phoenix Raceway! Proudly presented by Perfect Shine Auto Spa alongside official Season 16 presenting sponsor Clear Low Communications, tonight's race marks a vital chapter as drivers battle for playoff positioning and momentum.

The Phoenix Challenge: Dogleg Restarts & Tire Preservation

Phoenix Raceway is famous for its unique 1-mile tri-oval layout. Drivers face one of the most nerve-wracking maneuvers in all of sim racing: the dogleg restart. Expect 3-wide and even 4-wide chaos as trucks cut deep onto the apron trying to gain precious track position into Turn 3.

With 140 laps ahead, tire management will play a massive factor. Managing right-front tire wear through the long sweeps of Turns 1 and 2 will separate the contenders from the pretenders when late-race pit strategy unfolds.

Chase Championship Implications

With points leader Benjamin Lacy holding a strong position, contenders behind him like Scott Sanderson, Joshua Adams, Kevin Foster, and road-course victor Nick Nickerson are hungrier than ever to grab victory in the desert. Meanwhile, the bubble battle around 10th position remains razor-thin with Johnathon Platt and Eddie Hagigh separated by just a handful of points!
