Season 16 Chase Round 4 • Legacy Towing & Travis Auto Service 140 at Phoenix Raceway • Official Race Report

Overtime Desert Duel at Phoenix: Nick Nickerson (#2) Edges Benjamin Lacy (#7) by 0.134s While Lacy's 62-Point Night Sets Up a Two-Man Homestead Title Showdown!

In a 147-lap overtime thriller under the Arizona lights, Nick Nickerson (#2 | GFR Racing) leads 27 laps and holds off teammate Benjamin I. Lacy (#7) by 0.1342 seconds for his 5th win of Season 16—while Lacy's Pole, Stage 1 & 2 Sweep, 91 Laps Led, and 0x Clean-Race Bonus deliver a massive 62-point night that trims the Championship field to just Lacy and Scott Sanderson (#93) entering Homestead-Miami!

📺 SIMTRAX BROADCAST REPLAY: Watch the full broadcast of the Legacy Towing & Travis Auto Service 140 (Season 16 Chase Round 4 • Race 20 of 21) from Phoenix Raceway—where #2 Nick Nickerson and #7 Benjamin I. Lacy battled nose-to-tail across 147 laps of overtime desert action! Watch on YouTube →

AVONDALE, AZ — The penultimate round of the Season 16 Bandit Racing League Craftsman Truck Series Chase—the Legacy Towing and Travis Auto Service 140 at the 1.0-mile Phoenix Raceway open-dogleg oval—arrived with championship elimination stakes hanging over every restart. With 60 standard points on the table for next week's finale at Homestead-Miami, any contender who left the Arizona desert more than 60 points behind Championship leader Benjamin I. Lacy (#7 | GFR Racing) would see their title hopes mathematically extinguished.

What unfolded over 1 hour, 26 minutes, and 147 overtime laps (extended 7 laps past the scheduled 140-lap distance by 11 cautions for 33 laps) was an instant-classic duel between the two winningest drivers of Season 16: GFR Racing teammates Nick Nickerson (#2 | Ford F-150) and Benjamin I. Lacy (#7 | RAM). Starting side-by-side on the front row after qualifying just 0.0046 seconds apart, Lacy and Nickerson combined to lead 118 of the 147 laps. While Lacy swept the Pole (26.9746s), Stage 1, Stage 2, and a race-high 91 laps led with a spotless 0x incident sheet, Nickerson unleashed the Fastest Lap of the Race on Lap 125 (27.1093s), paced the field for 27 critical late-race laps, and fended off Lacy by 0.1342 seconds at the overtime checkered flag to capture his 5th victory of Season 16 (58 total points)—tying Lacy for the league lead in wins!

Even in finishing runner-up by a truck length, Benjamin Lacy (#7) walked out of Avondale as the night's biggest points earner, banking a staggering 62 total points (35 Race + 20 Stage Sweep + 1 Pole + 1 Lap Led + 5 Clean-Race 0x Bonus) to stretch his Championship lead to 46 points (2,290 to 2,244) over GFR Racing teammate Scott Sanderson (#93)—setting up an all-GFR Racing two-man showdown for the Season 16 Championship at Homestead-Miami!

Qualifying & Stage 1 (Laps 1–42): 0.0054s Top-Three Blanket & Lacy's Wire-to-Wire Opening

Single-truck qualifying at Phoenix produced one of the tightest front-row lockouts in Bandit Racing League history. Championship leader Benjamin I. Lacy (#7 | GFR Racing RAM) ripped off a 26.9746-second lap to capture his 4th Pole Position of Season 16 (+1 bonus point)—edging hometown Phoenix favorite Nick Nickerson (#2 | GFR Racing F-150, 26.9792s) by a microscopic 0.0046 seconds and championship challenger Scott Sanderson (#93 | GFR Racing Silverado, 26.9800s) by just 0.0054 seconds! Behind the all-GFR top three, ESR's Ethan Sikorski (#24, 27.0376s) and Jackson Knaak (#00, 27.0415s) locked down 4th and 5th ahead of 2x BRL Champion Kevin Foster (#8 | GFR Racing, 27.0421s)—who was celebrating his Milestone 200th Career BRL Start—and ZeroFoxtrot's Curtis Yancey (#51, 27.0572s) and Connor Gibson (#31, 27.0796s).

🟢 ALL-GFR FRONT ROW (SEPARATED BY 0.0046s): Pole-sitter #7 Benjamin I. Lacy (inside, 26.9746s) and #2 Nick Nickerson (outside, 26.9792s) bring the 19-truck field across the yellow-and-black Phoenix Raceway stripe ahead of #24 Ethan Sikorski, 200th-start honoree #8 Kevin Foster, #22 Joshua L. Adams, #93 Scott Sanderson, #31 Connor Gibson, and #00 Jackson Knaak!

When the green flag dropped for the 42-lap opening stage, the field settled into a blistering, 100% caution-free green-flag run from Lap 1 to Lap 42. Every driver in the top 13 recorded 0x incident points across the entire opening segment! Out front, Benjamin Lacy (#7) was untouchable, leading all 42 laps wire-to-wire with a 149.5 Driver Rating and 14 fastest laps to claim the Stage 1 Victory (+10 stage points) by 1.654 seconds over Nick Nickerson (#2, P2, +9 pts) and Scott Sanderson (#93, P3, +8 pts, 27.1545s fast lap on Lap 3). Jackson Knaak (#00, P4, +7 pts) and 937 Racing's Joshua L. Adams (#22, P5, +6 pts, 6 passes / 5 quality passes) rounded out the top five, followed by Curtis Yancey (#51, P6, +5 pts), Michael Rakes (#47, P7, +4 pts), Kevin Foster (#8, P8, +3 pts), Connor Gibson (#31, P9, +2 pts), and Jon Osborne (#11, P10, +1 pt after making 8 Stage 1 passes).

Stage 2 (Laps 43–84): Curtis Yancey Paces 19 Laps Before Lacy Completes the Stage Sweep

Following the Stage 1 break and pit road strategy decisions, the intensity ratcheted up in Stage 2 (37 laps of racing to the Lap 84 stage flag, interrupted by 3 cautions for 9 laps). ZeroFoxtrot's Curtis Yancey (#51) cycled to the front and showed tremendous short-run muscle over the Phoenix dogleg—leading 19 laps in Stage 2 (and 25 laps overall on the night while recording a race-high 23 total passes, 10 quality passes, and 5 closing passes!) while GFR's Scott Sanderson (#93) also led 2 laps and clocked a Stage 2 best of 27.2867s on Lap 52.

However, late-stage contact shuffled both Yancey and Sanderson back into the pack prior to the Lap 84 stage stripe, opening the door for Benjamin I. Lacy (#7) to retake command for 16 laps and capture the Stage 2 Checkered Flag (+10 stage points)! The sweep gave Lacy the maximum 20 stage points on the night and pushed his league-best total to 12 Stage Wins in Season 16. 937 Racing's Joshua L. Adams (#22) charged to 2nd in Stage 2 (+9 pts — 15 total stage points!) just 1.198 seconds behind Lacy, while Nick Nickerson (#2) made 12 passes in the segment to finish 3rd (+8 pts — 17 total stage points!) ahead of Tre Ellis (#63, P4, +7 pts, 9 passes, 0x), Peter Murphy (#38, P5, +6 pts, 9 passes, 0x), Jackson Knaak (#00, P6, +5 pts — 12 total stage points!), Jon Osborne (#11, P7, +4 pts), Michael Rakes (#47, P8, +3 pts — 7 total stage points), Matt Crockett (#15, P9, +2 pts), and Connor Gibson (#31, P10, +1 pt).

💨 TURN 1–2 TIRE SMOKE: After pacing 25 laps and recording a race-high 23 total passes, ZeroFoxtrot's #51 Curtis Yancey loops it in Turns 1–2 under the Sunoco scoring pylon while #12 Bill Harkins, #31 Connor Gibson, and #47 Michael Rakes thread the needle past! Yancey rallied back to finish 7th on the lead lap.
🔥 HARD CHARGER CHARGE: ZeroFoxtrot's #31 Connor Gibson (Kellogg's / Got Milk? Silverado) dives to the inside behind Roundy Motorsports' #12 Bill Harkins (P14, 147 laps completed) on his way from 8th on the grid to a Season 16 Chase-best 4th-place finish (-0.5505s, +4 Hard Charger tie)!

Final Stage & 147-Lap Overtime Shootout: Nickerson vs. Lacy at the Stripe!

The final stage turned into an all-out desert street fight across the open dogleg as 7 cautions repeatedly bunched the field for double-file restarts and pushed the race 7 laps into overtime (147 total laps). During the pit and restart exchanges, Peter Murphy (#38 | RAM) led 2 laps and Curtis Yancey (#51) led 6 more laps before the battle for the trophy distilled down to the front-row starters: #2 Nick Nickerson and #7 Benjamin Lacy.

With fresh rubber bolted on for the closing run, Nickerson (#2) blistered the 1-mile oval with the Fastest Lap of the Entire Race on Lap 125 (27.1093s)—his 17th fastest lap of the evening—and seized control at the front of the field for 27 laps. Through the final overtime restart, Lacy threw everything he had at his GFR Racing teammate while keeping his #7 RAM completely clean (0x incidents over 147 laps!). Diving off Turn 4 to the checkered flag on Lap 147, Nickerson held off Lacy by 0.1342 seconds to claim his 5th victory of Season 16 and 55th career BRL victory!

Right on their rear bumpers, ESR's Jackson Knaak (#00) capped off a brilliant 2x drive (17 passes, 9 quality passes) just 0.3836 seconds back in 3rd place (46 total points) to vault into 4th in the Championship Chase! ZeroFoxtrot's Connor Gibson (#31) carved from 8th on the grid to finish a Chase-best 4th (-0.5505s, 4x incidents, 36 pts), while tonight's Driver Spotlight—937 Racing's Joshua L. Adams (#22)—rallied from 9th to finish 5th (-0.7973s, 19 passes, 10 quality passes, 4x incidents, 47 total points) right ahead of 937 Racing teammate Michael Rakes (#47, Q10 → P6, -1.0562s, 4x incidents, 38 pts)!

Franchise Team Financials, Sponsor Bonuses & Viewer Fantasy Report

On the franchise balance sheet, GFR Racing turned Phoenix into a desert goldmine—scoring 140 team points, capturing their 13th victory in 20 races (2,864 total team points), and unlocking a +$34,619 Apex Fuel Virtual Sponsorship Bonus (7.1 average finish over 2 weeks) to bank +$269,619 in gross earnings (+$248,512 net profit after -$21,107 in travel/prep) and push their league-leading treasury to $4,227,984!

Behind GFR, the battle for 4th place in the Franchise Team Standings saw a major shakeup: ZeroFoxtrot racked up 81 team points behind #31 Connor Gibson (P4), #51 Curtis Yancey (P7, 25 laps led), and #11 Jon Osborne (5 stage pts, P19) to bank +$120,000 gross (+$94,135 net, $1,901,948 balance) and leapfrog Roundy Motorsports into 4th overall (1,129 pts to 1,117 pts)! However, Roundy Motorsports scored 47 team points (#15 Matt Crockett P13, #12 Bill Harkins P14) and unlocked a +$32,627 Checkered Flag Media Virtual Sponsorship Bonus (11.2 avg finish over 2 weeks) for a +$102,627 gross haul (+$80,344 net, $2,328,959 balance)—while Ethan Sikorski Racing (ESR) poured in 83 team points (#00 Knaak P3, #24 Sikorski P9, #21 Santos P16) and +$130,000 gross (+$100,167 net, $2,397,095 balance) to close to within 45 points of Roundy Motorsports at 1,072 points! 937 Racing cemented 2nd in the team standings (1,502 pts, $2,729,535 balance) with 63 team points and +$85,000 gross (+$63,880 net) from Adams (P5) and Rakes (P6), while Legacy Racing holds 3rd (1,385 pts, $2,158,680 balance) after banking +$65,000 gross (+$42,544 net) behind Davis Carroll (P15) and Nicole Kriesel (P17).

In the Weekly Viewer Fantasy Challenge, Matt Crockett stayed red-hot—winning his second consecutive weekly fantasy crown (and league-leading 5th weekly win of Season 16) with 152 points behind a four-driver roster of Nick Nickerson, Benjamin Lacy, Jackson Knaak, and Curtis Yancey! Bhark24 and Lady Chaos tied for 2nd on the week with 143 points apiece (picking Nickerson, Lacy, Sanderson, and Sikorski), while overall fantasy leader Rebekah Weaver scored 138 points (Lacy, Adams, Rakes, Sanderson) to take a 170-point lead (1,933 to 1,763) over Crockett into next week's Homestead-Miami finale!
