Old Guard vs. New Blood: The Season 16 Chase for the Championship Preview 🏁🔥

Presented by Clear Low Communications • SimRacerHub Historical Deep-Dive • October 18, 2026

⚡ 16 RACES DOWN. 10 CONTENDERS LOCKED IN. 5 RACES FOR IMMORTALITY.

After 16 grueling regular-season battles—capped off by Benjamin Lacy’s statement victory at Martinsville Speedway—the Season 16 Bandit Racing League Craftsman Truck Series Chase Grid is officially set! For the next five weeks, the top 10 drivers in the standings will have their points reset and go head-to-head across every discipline in sim racing to crown the Season 16 Champion.

A deep dive into the official Bandit Racing League SimRacerHub archives (spanning nearly 200 league races from Season 2 through Season 16) reveals two extraordinary storylines: Every single one of Season 16's 16 regular-season races was won by a driver inside this Top 10 Chase field, and the grid is split right down the middle—5 Battle-Tested Playoff Veterans & Past Champions vs. 5 First-Time Main-Series Chase Contenders!

📜 How the Season 16 Chase Works (Rulebook Section 2)

Unlike elimination brackets where one bad night ends your season, the Bandit Racing League Season 16 Chase is a pure, 5-race championship gauntlet with NO elimination rounds:

  • Qualification: Strictly the Top 10 drivers in regular-season points following Race 16 at Martinsville.
  • No Eliminations: All 10 Chase qualifiers compete in all five Chase events (Races 17 through 21).
  • Points Reset & Seeding Separation: Regular-season dominance is rewarded on the reset ladder—the #1 Seed starts at 2,100 points, the #2 Seed starts at 2,075 (-25), the #3 Seed starts at 2,065 (-35), and Seeds #4 through #10 are separated by 5 points each down to 2,030.
  • Crowning the Champion: The Chase driver with the highest cumulative point total after the checkered flag at Homestead-Miami Speedway on November 18 wins the Season 16 Championship!

What Happened in Round 1 & What’s at Stake at Kansas Speedway

Talladega blew the Season 16 title fight wide open! While Roundy Motorsports owner/driver Matt Crockett (#15) played ultimate spoiler by winning his 1st career BRL race by 0.010 seconds over Chase contender Jackson Knaak (#00), late-race superspeedway carnage compressed the top of the Chase standings. Scott Sanderson (#93) finished 9th while #1 seed Benjamin Lacy (#7) was collected in a Lap 58 crash (finishing 14th)—slicing Lacy’s points lead from 25 points down to just 3 points (2,119 to 2,116)! Meanwhile, Dylan McDonald (#9) bagged 18 stage points and finished 6th to score a race-high 53 points and vault three spots into 4th overall (2,095 pts, -24).

Now the 10-driver grid heads to Kansas Speedway (110 Laps)—where earlier in Race 11 (Sep 9), #8 Chase contender Michael Rakes (#47) stretched his fuel tank to win over Kevin Foster (#8, P2), while Benjamin Lacy (#7, P5) led a race-high 40 laps, Nick Nickerson (#2, P6) led 13 laps, Connor Gibson (#31) took 7th, and Joshua Adams (#22, P8) led 14 laps. For Scott Sanderson (#93, P26 in Kansas 1) and Dylan McDonald (#9, P25 in Kansas 1), Wednesday night is the ultimate high-line test to prove they can contend for the title on 1.5-mile intermediate asphalt!

⚔️ The Franchise & Generational War: By The Numbers

🏆 THE OLD GUARD (5 VETERANS)

Lacy (#7), Foster (#8), Nickerson (#2), Knaak (#00), and Gibson (#31) combine for 756+ recorded SimRacerHub starts, 84+ wins, and 7 League Championships (Nickerson 4x, Foster 2x, Lacy 1x), while multi-time playoff veterans Knaak and Gibson hunt for their elusive first BRL crown.

🏢 FRANCHISE SUPREMACY

GFR Racing dominates the grid with 5 Chase trucks (half the field: #7, #93, #8, #2, #13) and 10 regular-season wins, while 937 Racing locks in 2 Chase trucks (#22, #47). Single-truck challengers ESR (#00), Legacy Racing (#9), and ZeroFoxtrot (#31) look to spoil the party.
