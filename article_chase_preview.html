📜 How the Season 16 Chase Works (Rulebook Section 2)

Unlike elimination brackets where one bad night ends your season, the Bandit Racing League Season 16 Chase is a pure, 5-race championship gauntlet with NO elimination rounds:

  • Qualification: Strictly the Top 10 drivers in regular-season points following Race 16 at Martinsville.
  • No Eliminations: All 10 Chase qualifiers compete in all five Chase events (Races 17 through 21).
  • Points Reset & Seeding Separation: Regular-season dominance is rewarded on the reset ladder—the #1 Seed starts at 2,100 points, the #2 Seed starts at 2,075 (-25), the #3 Seed starts at 2,065 (-35), and Seeds #4 through #10 are separated by 5 points each down to 2,030.
  • Crowning the Champion: The Chase driver with the highest cumulative point total after the checkered flag at Homestead-Miami Speedway on November 18 wins the Season 16 Championship!

What Happened at Kansas & Why Mid-Ohio Could Flip the Championship

Round 2 at Kansas Speedway delivered two completely different races in one night! The early going was smooth and green, as Joshua L. Adams (#22 | 937 Racing) set a blistering pace—rocketing out to a 5-second lead early and sweeping both Stage 1 (leading all 33 laps) and Stage 2 (leading 28 of 33 laps) on his way to a race-high 66 laps led and 54 points scored, vaulting him into 2nd place in the Chase (2,156 pts, -14). Then Stage 3 turned into a caution-filled chess match (3 cautions for 13 laps, forcing 2 Green-White-Checkered overtime attempts), culminating in a breathtaking final-lap battle between GFR Racing teammates Nick Nickerson (#2) and Benjamin Lacy (#7). Starting 19th on the grid (+18 spots), Nickerson pulled off a masterpiece of a pass on the final lap to beat Lacy by 0.243 seconds, with Kevin Foster (#8, P3) completing an all-0x 1-2-3 GFR Racing podium sweep!

Now the 10-driver Chase grid heads to Mid-Ohio Sports Car Course (45 Laps • 13 Turns • 2.258 Miles) in Lexington, Ohio! Moving Round 3 from Road America to Mid-Ohio creates a massive championship curveball: in BRL's only prior visit to Mid-Ohio (Season 12), Nick Nickerson (#2) won the Pole, led 39 of 40 laps, and logged 0x incidents to WIN (and he also dominated S16 Watkins Glen from the pole, leading 40 of 52 laps!). Conversely, current points leader Benjamin Lacy (#7) finished 20th (Q23, 9x) in S12 at Mid-Ohio and 11th at S16 Watkins Glen. Meanwhile, Scott Sanderson (#93, P3 & Stage 1 Winner at WGI), Jackson Knaak (#00, P7 at S12 Mid-Ohio & P4 at S16 WGI), Dylan McDonald (#9, P5 at WGI), Kevin Foster (#8, P10 at S12 Mid-Ohio & P7 at WGI), and road-course sleeper Connor Gibson (#31, P5 with 0x at S12 Mid-Ohio!) all see Lexington as their golden opportunity to strike!
