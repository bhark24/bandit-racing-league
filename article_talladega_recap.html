Six Years & 162 Starts in the Making: Matt Crockett Captures 1st Career BRL Victory by 0.010 Seconds at Talladega!

In his 121st Main-Series start (162nd overall BRL start since January 2020), Roundy Motorsports Owner/Driver Matt Crockett (#15) overcomes a mid-race spin to lead the only lap that mattered—edging Jackson Knaak (#00) and Brandon Jackson (#91) in a 0.010-second photo finish!

Smoke show in Alabama: Matt Crockett (#15 Roundy Motorsports Ford F-150) burns it down in front of a packed Talladega grandstand to celebrate his first career Bandit Racing League victory!

LINCOLN, AL — For more than six years, eleven seasons, and over 17,200 laps of Bandit Racing League competition, Matt Crockett showed up week after week, built franchises, mentored teammates, and battled through the pack in search of that elusive first trip to BRL Victory Lane. On Wednesday night under the lights at the 2.66-mile Talladega Superspeedway—in the opening round of the Season 16 Chase for the Championship—the wait finally came to a breathtaking end.

Driving his signature pink-and-black #15 Roundy Motorsports Ford F-150, Crockett survived a Lap 37 backstretch spin, rallied from 19th at the end of Stage 2, and rode a massive high-lane draft push from Brandon Jackson (#91) out of the tri-oval on the 73rd and final lap. Sparks flying against the outside wall, Crockett surged ahead in the final yards to beat Chase contender Jackson Knaak (#00, ESR) to the stripe by just 0.0105 seconds (0.010s)—with Brandon Jackson crossing third just 0.0369 seconds back in one of the closest three-wide photo finishes in Bandit Racing League history!

How the #15 Conquered Talladega: Spun on Lap 37, Victorious on Lap 73

Crockett rolled off the grid in 14th place (54.7857s) and immediately showed draft speed, ripping off the fastest lap of his night on Lap 3 (51.9342 seconds) and methodically working the pack to finish 10th in Stage 1 (+1 stage point).

Disaster nearly struck on Lap 37 during Stage 2. While running in the draft down the backstretch, Car #62 (Ty Corino)—who appeared to be experiencing an issue—turned down directly into Crockett's #15 truck, spinning Crockett out and triggering a caution (resulting in an EOL penalty for #62). Crockett dropped back to 19th place at the conclusion of Stage 2 (Lap 42) with 13x incident points on his ledger and repairs required on pit road.

Undeterred, Crockett went to work in the final 31 laps. Across the 73-lap overtime affair, SimRacerHub telemetry credited Crockett with 36 total passes, 12 quality passes (passes of trucks running inside the Top 15), 6 closing passes in the final laps, a 10.7 average running position, and a stellar 107.4 Driver Rating. When the field stacked up two- and three-wide on the overtime dash to the checkered flag, Crockett positioned his #15 on the high side with Brandon Jackson (#91) locked onto his rear bumper. As Jackson Knaak (#00) held the bottom lane through the tri-oval, Jackson's relentless push propelled Crockett's #15 alongside and ahead by mere inches at the scoring loop—making Lap 73 the only lap Crockett led all night!

To make the night even sweeter for Roundy Motorsports, team co-driver Bill Harkins (#12) stormed from 24th on the starting grid to finish 10th (+14 positions gained), placing two Roundy Motorsports trucks inside the Top 10 and vaulting the franchise to 957 team points with its first team victory of Season 16!

Talladega Stewards Log & Wall of Shame Update

Talladega kept Race Control busy with 5 official EOL penalties across 73 laps—headlined by a 2x EOL double-feature from Ty Corino (#62) on Laps 37 and 58, plus EOL rulings on Ethan Sikorski (#24, Lap 21), Matthew Bailey (#54, Lap 54), and Bob Berry (#75, Lap 64 + possible ARCA braking note). Race Control also issued a new league-wide policy evaluation regarding communicating truck damage status over chat.
