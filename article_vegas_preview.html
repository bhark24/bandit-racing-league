🎰 High Rollers Hit the Nevada Tri-Oval

The Bandit Racing League Craftsman Truck Series charges into Nevada this Wednesday, September 23rd, for Race 13 of Season 16: The Las Vegas 110 at the famed Las Vegas Motor Speedway. Proudly presented by league partner Clear Low Communications and event sponsor Liquid Death Mountain Water, this 110-lap spectacle promises high-speed draft trains, wide-open racing, and intense pit strategy games under the desert lights.

Broadcast live starting at 9:00 PM EST on SimTrax Broadcasting (YouTube), drivers face a grueling 1.5-mile D-shaped oval with 20 degrees of progressive banking. As tires wear down on the abrasive surface, keeping your truck underneath you while executing mandatory green-flag pit stops will make or break your night.

With just four races left before the postseason reset, every single position, stage point, and caution restart carries maximum urgency.

⛽ 70% Fuel Cap Mechanics: 3 Mandatory Pit Stops

Under Season 16 technical rules, fuel capacities are restricted to 70% capacity (12.6 gallons max limit). At full throttle around Las Vegas Motor Speedway, Craftsman Trucks burn roughly 0.47 gallons per lap, limiting maximum fuel range to approximately 26.8 laps.

To complete the 110-lap race distance, competitors will need 4 distinct fuel stints, making 3 green-flag pit stops mandatory. Drivers who draft effectively can stretch fuel stints to 27 or 28 laps, potentially gaining track position over early pitters.

🛞 The Tactical Gamble: 2 Tire Sets Total

The ultimate strategic variable at Las Vegas is the strict 2 Sets of Tires Total limit (1 starting set on the truck + 1 fresh set in the pit stall). Because drivers must pit 3 times for fuel, everyone will be forced to complete at least two Fuel-Only stops.

  • The Halfway Strategy: Taking fresh rubber on Pit Stop 2 (around Lap 55) splits tire wear evenly at ~55 laps per set.
  • The Late-Race Aggression: Saving your single tire set for Pit Stop 3 (around Lap 80) leaves you on worn rubber early, but unlocks a 30-lap closing charge on fresh tires where you can gain up to 1.5–2.0 seconds per lap over rivals on old tires!

Throw in 1 Fast Repair safety net for emergency chassis restoration, and pit road will be absolute prime-time theater.
