VIVA LAS VEGAS 110 RECAP

Season 16 • Race 13 • Las Vegas Motor Speedway

Race Update: High speeds and intense tire/fuel management at Las Vegas Motor Speedway culminated in a dramatic night on the 1.5-mile tri-oval. Race Stewards executed major post-race administrative reviews, resulting in a Double Disqualification for intentional wrecking under caution.

The Bandit Racing League Craftsman Truck Series tackled the high banks of Las Vegas Motor Speedway for Race 13 of Season 16 in the Viva Las Vegas 110. Operating under strict technical rules featuring a 70% fuel capacity limit (12.6 gallons max) and a 2-tire set limit, drivers were forced to balance blistering corner speeds with tire conservation across 110 intense laps.

Race Control Audit & Double Disqualification

While the green flag action featured intense multi-groove drafting and strategy maneuvers, the biggest headlines of the night came from Race Control following a late-race yellow flag at Lap 96.

🚨 BRL Stewards Ruling: Double Disqualification

Following Lap 96 caution review, telemetric evidence and steward audits confirmed mutual Intentional Wrecking under yellow flag conditions between Car #9 (Dylan McDonald) and Car #18 (Victor Weaver).

  • Car #9 (Dylan McDonald): DISQUALIFIED from the event (forfeits all points). Due to active probation status, McDonald receives a 1-Week Suspension, Season-Long Probation, 3 Weeks of EOL Starts, and a Lap 1 Drive-Through Penalty upon return.
  • Car #18 (Victor Weaver): DISQUALIFIED from the event (forfeits all points). Weaver is placed on 3-Week Probation and assigned an EOL (End of Line) Start for his next event.

Additional On-Track Rulings

  • Lap 74: Car #14 (Jason Allegrini) received an EOL Penalty after getting loose and pushing Car #91 into the outside wall.
  • Lap 78: Car #62 (Ty Corino) issued an Official Warning for failing to hold his restart line, triggering a rear-end checkup for Car #31.
  • Lap 85: Car #99 black flag cleared after being collected under yellow.
  • Lap 101: Car #36 (Adam Tahan) issued an EOL Penalty after attempting to squeeze Car #47 onto the apron and spinning himself.
  • Lap 108: Car #24 issued an EOL Penalty for a late-race side collision into Car #47.
