September 16, 2026 • Texas Motor Speedway

Dylan McDonald Charges from 23rd to Win "The Lone Star State 120" at Texas!

High-speed drafting, pit road miscommunications, multi-car accordion crashes, and post-race drama highlight an incredible night of racing in Texas.

"From 23rd on the grid to victory lane! Dylan McDonald in the #9 truck executed a masterful drive to claim victory at Texas Motor Speedway in The Lone Star State 120."

Texas Speed & High-Banked Drama

Texas Motor Speedway lived up to its high-speed reputation as the Bandit Racing League staged 120 laps of relentless Craftsman Truck Series action in "The Lone Star State 120". With stages set at Laps 36 and 72, drivers faced intense restart battles, high-risk 3-wide moves, and split-second check-ups across the quad-oval.

Race Breakdown & Key Moments

Action started immediately on Lap 2 when Car #63 attempted to leave room up top while in the middle of a 3-wide pack, turning himself across the nose of Car #00. Race Control assessed an early Drive-Through penalty to the #63 truck.

On Lap 12 under green flag conditions, Car #54 scraped the outside wall, with Car #37 following into the fence and subsequently getting tagged by Car #69, who failed to lift or avoid the stackup. On Lap 23, Car #15 suffered a sudden mechanical steering/wheel hardware failure, sending him hard into the wall and incurring an EOL penalty.

Stage 1 concluded at Lap 36 with green-white checkered points awarded. Segment 2 saw friction on Lap 56 during pit entry when Car #12 braked to pit; Car #54 missed the "pitting in" call and collided with #12 due to lack of reaction time (ruled a racing deal).

The most complex incident occurred on Lap 62 off Turn 4 when Car #00 scraped the wall in front of Car #47. In the resulting check-up, Car #35 forced Car #93 into the grass to avoid and slowed down, causing Car #24 to tag #35 in a multi-car accordion stackup. Stewards ruled it a multi-vehicle racing incident with no single driver at fault.

After Stage 2 completed at Lap 72, final segment restarts escalated intensity. On Lap 79, Cars #50 and #17 made contact when #17 drifted up off Turn 2 (EOL penalty to #17). On Lap 84, Car #6 checked up sharply on the quad-oval after a restart, giving Car #24 no reaction time; Car #6 received an EOL penalty, and Car #36 filed a formal protest. Finally, post-race cool-down laps saw Car #36 intentionally drive into Car #24, resulting in an Official Post-Race Conduct Warning issued to #36.

⚠️ BRL Race Control Rulings & Penalties

  • Car #63 (Drive-Through Penalty): Lap 2 3-wide chop across #00 nose.
  • Car #15 (EOL Penalty): Lap 23 mechanical wheel hardware failure.
  • Car #17 (EOL Penalty): Lap 79 Turn 2 corner drifting contact.
  • Car #6 (EOL Penalty & Formal Protest Noted): Lap 84 quad-oval restart check-up wreck (Protest filed by #36).
  • Car #36 (Official Warning): Post-race intentional contact into #24 on cool-down.
