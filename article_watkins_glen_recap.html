WATKINS GLEN ROAD COURSE SHOWDOWN

Season 16 • Race 14 • Watkins Glen International • Sep 30, 2026

Race Summary: Nick Nickerson put on a masterclass at Watkins Glen International, dominating the road course like the G.O.A.T. he is. Benjamin Lacy locked down 2nd for a GFR Racing 1-2 sweep, Scott Sanderson grabbed 3rd, and Dylan McDonald produced the drive of the night charging from dead last to 5th!

Masterclass at The Glen

The iconic elevation changes, the technical Bus Stop chicane, and the high-speed sweeping turns of Watkins Glen International set the stage for one of the most anticipated road course battles of Season 16.

From the drop of the green flag, Nick Nickerson (#2) showed absolute dominance. Leading 41 of the 45 laps, Nickerson carved up the apexes with flawless precision, proving why he is heralded as the G.O.A.T. of road course competition in the Bandit Racing League.

GFR Racing 1-2 Finish & Podiums

Championship leader Benjamin Lacy (#13) drove a clean, tactical race to bring home 2nd place, securing a commanding 1-2 finish for GFR Racing and bolstering their massive lead in the Franchise Team Standings. Scott Sanderson (#93) of 937 Racing capped off a hard-fought evening with a podium finish in 3rd place.

Drive of the Night: Dylan McDonald's Dead-Last Recovery

The standout story of the race belonged to Dylan McDonald (#1). Starting from dead last (P24) due to a required End of Line (EOL) penalty from previous administrative rulings, McDonald launched an aggressive comeback drive through the field. Navigating heavy traffic and technical chicane maneuvers, McDonald clawed his way back to finish 5th place—earning weekly Spotlight honors!
