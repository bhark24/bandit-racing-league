Position #1 • Race Control & Live Officiating

Live Race Control Director / Official

The Bandit Racing League is seeking an experienced, impartial Live Race Control Official to command the tower during our official weekly league events. Because live officiating directly impacts competition integrity and broadcast quality, this is a paid position reserved for candidates who can provide a proven track record of iRacing Race Control experience along with verifiable league references.

🏁 Key Responsibilities
  • Command the Tower: Oversee live race sessions from green flag to checkered flag as a dedicated, non-driving official.
  • Blocked / Unsafe Track Cautions & Lineup Management: iRacing handles automated caution flags, except when Race Control must throw a manual caution because the track is blocked or unsafe. Verify field lineups, manage lucky dogs/wave-arounds, and keep caution laps accurate.
  • Live Rulebook Enforcement: Issue or clear in-sim penalties/black flags (pit road speeding, jump starts, Blend Line violations, EOL penalties) strictly according to the Bandit Racing League Rulebook.
  • Driver & Broadcaster Coordination: Maintain calm, clear, and professional communication over iRacing voice/text chat and Discord while coordinating live with the broadcaster.
  • Incident Logging: Record lap numbers and cars involved in on-track incidents during the race to hand off for post-race review and the Wall of Shame report.
📋 Required Qualifications (Must Be Verifiable)
  • Proven Officiating Resume: Documented history serving as a Race Director or Live Race Control Admin in established iRacing oval leagues or broadcast series.
  • Verifiable References: Must provide contact information (Discord handle, email, or league owner/broadcaster reference) from previous leagues or broadcast partners who can vouch for your reliability, composure, and fairness.
  • Mastery of iRacing Admin Commands: Complete fluency with iRacing session admin commands (!black, !clear, !eol, !yellow, wave-arounds, and lineup adjustments) under live pressure.
  • 100% Impartiality: Zero bias toward any driver or team on the grid.
⏰ Schedule: Weekly League Race Nights (Dedicated Non-Driving Official)

Position #2 • Social Media & Content

Social Media & Content Coordinator

The Bandit Racing League is looking for an enthusiastic, creative Social Media & Content Coordinator to help showcase our racing action, drivers, and weekly storylines across social platforms. This is a volunteer crew position ideal for an active league member or sim-racing fan who enjoys creating content, building hype around race night, and helping grow the Bandit Racing League community.

📱 Key Responsibilities
  • Weekly Race Promotion: Post upcoming race previews, schedule reminders, and broadcast links leading up to race night.
  • Race Results & Standings Recaps: Share official podium winners, Victory Lane graphics, championship standings updates, and Wall of Shame highlights after each event.
  • Short-Form Clips & Highlights: Pull exciting finishes, battles, saves, and big moments from broadcasts or iRacing replays to share on TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Reels, Facebook, or X.
  • Community Engagement: Spotlight driver milestones, paint schemes, and upcoming season announcements (like Season 17) to keep current drivers engaged and attract clean new racers.
✨ What We're Looking For
  • Social Media & Content Savvy: Familiarity with posting on platforms like Facebook, X (Twitter), Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube Shorts.
  • Basic Graphic or Video Clipping Skills: Comfortable using tools like Canva, Photoshop, or basic video clipping software (or sharing existing graphics/articles generated on the BRL website).
  • Consistent Weekly Presence: Able to dedicate a little time each week before and after race night to keep our social pages active and current.
  • Team Player: Positive ambassador for the Bandit Racing League brand in the wider iRacing community.
⏰ Schedule: Flexible Weekly (Active Drivers & Community Members Welcome)
🟢 Volunteer Crew Position ⚙️ League Operations & Driver Relations
Position #3 • League Administration & Stewarding

League Administrator / Operations Admin

The Bandit Racing League is seeking dependable, level-headed League Administrators to help manage day-to-day league operations, assist drivers, and keep weekly sessions and post-race procedures running smoothly. This is a volunteer crew position suited for trusted league members who care about fair competition, clear communication, and strong community standards.

⚙️ Key Responsibilities
  • Driver Onboarding & Roster Support: Help welcome new applicants in Discord, verify car numbers/registrations, and answer questions about league rules and race-night procedures.
  • Session & Schedule Setup Support: Assist with weekly practice/race session checks, weather/setup verification, and pre-race drivers' meeting announcements.
  • Post-Race Review & Steward Panel: Participate in post-race incident reviews, evaluate replay clips objectively against the Bandit Racing League Rulebook, and vote on penalty/Wall of Shame determinations (automatically recusing yourself from any incident involving your own car or team).
  • Discord & Community Moderation: Help keep Discord channels organized, constructive, and respectful throughout the week.
✨ What We're Looking For
  • Fairness & Objectivity: Able to separate on-track competition from admin duties and make impartial decisions by the book.
  • Strong Knowledge of the BRL Rulebook: Familiar with Bandit Racing League rules, point structures, and racing etiquette.
  • Active & Approachable: Regularly active on Discord and available around race nights to support the grid.
  • Team-First Mindset: Willing to collaborate with fellow admins and league leadership to continually improve the league.
⏰ Schedule: Weekly League Operations & Post-Race Review (Active Drivers Welcome)
💰 Commission-Based Compensation (Per Secured Sponsorship) 🤝 Partnerships & League Growth
Position #4 • Marketing & Sponsorships

Marketing & Sponsorship Coordinator

The Bandit Racing League is seeking a driven, results-oriented Marketing & Sponsorship Coordinator to connect the league with title sponsors, race-entitlement partners, and brand supporters. This is a performance-based, commission-compensated position where compensation is negotiated per sponsorship package and paid directly upon the successful completion and funding of each sponsorship agreement.

💰 Compensation & Payout Terms
  • Negotiated Per-Sponsorship Commission: Compensation is agreed upon on a per-sponsorship basis depending on the size and scope of the partnership (single-race entitlement, multi-race package, or full-season title sponsorship).
  • Definition of a "Locked-In" Sponsorship: A sponsorship is considered officially locked in once the sponsorship agreement is finalized and all sponsorship funds have been received and verified in full by the Bandit Racing League.
  • 10-Day Payout Guarantee: Once a sponsorship contract is financially fulfilled and all funds have cleared verification, your agreed-upon commission will be issued within 10 calendar days.
🤝 Key Responsibilities
  • Sponsor Outreach & Acquisition: Identify, pitch, and secure businesses, sim-racing brands, or community partners interested in sponsoring Bandit Racing League broadcasts, weekly races, or seasonal championships.
  • Partnership Proposals: Present league viewership, website traffic, social reach, and broadcast branding opportunities to prospective sponsors.
  • Sponsor Fulfillment Coordination: Work with league leadership and the broadcaster to ensure all promised sponsor logos, reads, and website placements are delivered professionally.
⏰ Payout Window: Issued within 10 days of verified sponsorship funding
