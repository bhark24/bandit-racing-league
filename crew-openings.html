Step Up for a Role ↓
🟡 Per-Race Stipend or Volunteer 🔥 High Priority Opening
Race Control & Live Officiating

Live Race Control / Race Admin

Serve in the tower during official Sunday night Bandit Racing League events to keep cautions crisp, restarts accurate, and race flow smooth from green to checkered.

⏰ Time Commitment: Sunday Nights (approx. 8:45 PM – 10:15 PM EST during race sessions)
What You'll Do:
  • Monitor live race sessions from Race Control and manage caution periods, lucky dogs, and wave-arounds.
  • Issue or clear in-sim black flags when warranted under the official BRL Rulebook.
  • Coordinate live with drivers on Discord and with the SimTrax broadcast booth.
  • Log key lap numbers of on-track incidents for post-race steward review.
Non-driving tower role on race night
🟢 Volunteer Crew ⚡ Multiple Spots Open
Officiating & Wall of Shame Review

Post-Race Incident Review Steward

Join the impartial Steward Panel that reviews weekly race incidents, votes on penalty determinations, and powers the official BRL Wall of Shame reports.

⏰ Time Commitment: Flexible async schedule (Sunday post-race through Tuesday evening)
What You'll Do:
  • Review replay files or incident clips from the weekly race and evaluate driver responsibility.
  • Cast objective, rulebook-backed votes on warnings, point deductions, or EOL penalties.
  • Recuse yourself automatically from any incident in which your own car or team is involved.
  • Help ensure consistent, transparent officiating across the entire grid.
Active drivers & non-drivers welcome
🔵 Volunteer or Per-Project Bonus 📹 Content & Clips
Media, Growth & Highlights

Social Media & Highlights Creator

Showcase the action of the Bandit Racing League! Turn photo finishes, big saves, and wild Wall of Shame moments into shareable clips and weekly social posts.

⏰ Time Commitment: Flexible weekly (2–3 short clips or posts per week)
What You'll Do:
  • Clip exciting finishes, battles, and incidents from SimTrax YouTube broadcasts or iRacing replays.
  • Post short-form highlights and race-day countdowns to TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, X, or Facebook.
  • Spotlight driver storylines, championship battles, and upcoming Season 17 news.
Great for clip editors & creators
