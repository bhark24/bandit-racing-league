CONTROVERSY AT DAYTONA

Sean Britt Stripped of Win • Dylan McDonald Declared Winner • Race Report

Breaking News: League administration has intervened following a highly aggressive last-lap collision at the start-finish line, stripping the initial race winner of the victory and declaring Dylan McDonald the official winner of the Season 16 opener.

The Season 16 opener at Daytona International Speedway will go down in history as one of the most dramatic and controversial finishes the Bandit Racing League has ever seen.

Coming out of turn four on the final lap, Sean Britt and Jonathon Platt were locked in a furious drag race down to the start-finish line with the checkered flag in sight. In a desperate bid for the win, Britt made an extremely aggressive move down the track, crashing hard into the side of Platt's truck. The contact sent Platt spinning down into the infield grass in a cloud of smoke, while Britt crossed the finish line and was initially recorded as the race winner.

However, the celebration in Victory Lane was cut short.

Admin Review Intervenes

Following an immediate post-race administrative review, league officials determined that Britt's blocking and aggressive contact crossed the line of clean racing and violated league rules. As a result, Sean Britt was stripped of the victory.

Under league regulations, the win was awarded to the next highest-finishing eligible truck, officially declaring Dylan McDonald the winner of the Daytona opener. This victory marks a spectacular debut for the newly formed Legacy Racing team, placing them immediately in the championship spotlight.

Platt Denied Redress

Despite being the victim of the aggressive contact, Jonathon Platt was not rewarded the victory. League officials ruled the contact a racing incident rather than an intentional wreck, scoring Platt exactly where his truck slid across the finish line.

The decision has sparked intense debate throughout the paddock, but one thing is certain: Legacy Racing starts the season in Victory Lane, and the rivalry between Sean Britt and Jonathon Platt is just getting started as the series heads to Atlanta.
