Twenty races are in the books. Four grueling Chase rounds—from the Talladega pack, to a double-overtime thriller at Kansas, to 50 surgical laps at Mid-Ohio, to an instant-classic 0.1342-second desert overtime finish at Phoenix—have whittled the Season 16 Bandit Racing League field down to one final night under the South Florida lights at Homestead-Miami Speedway.

With a maximum of 67 points on the table in Wednesday night's 110-lap finale (40 Win + 20 Stage Sweep + 5 Clean Race 0x + 2 Led/Most Laps Led), the 60-point Homestead Championship window has slammed shut on eight of the ten Chase contenders. Only two drivers remain mathematically alive for the Season 16 Crown—and both drive under the GFR Racing banner.

👑 The Championship Picture: Who's In & Who's Out

🟢 WHO'S IN (2 MATHEMATICALLY ALIVE)

All-GFR Racing Title Showdown

1st: #7 Benjamin I. Lacy (GFR Racing) — 2,290 PTS

Magic Number: 22 Points. Lacy's 62-point masterclass at Phoenix (Pole, Stage 1 & 2 Sweep, 91 Laps Led, P2, 0x) built a massive 46-point cushion. He clinches his 2nd Career BRL Championship by scoring 22+ points at Homestead (finishing P19 or better with 0 stage pts, or P24+ with a 0x clean race), even if Sanderson scores the maximum 67 points!

Needs a Miracle + Perfection. The only driver inside the 60-point Homestead cutline (2,230 pts). To pull off one of the greatest upsets in BRL history, Sanderson must score at least 47+ points (essentially Win + Stage bonus + Laps Led) and hope Lacy suffers catastrophic trouble finishing 20th or worse without stage or clean-race points.
