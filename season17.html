🔥 NEW SEASON 17 HEADLINE ATTRACTION • WIN OR NOTHING

🤠 THE BANDIT BACK-ROW OUTLAW CHALLENGE

Something NASCAR doesn't do: every week in Season 17, the Previous Week's Winner and the Pole Winner have the chance to drop to the tail of the field via Race Control !eol and charge from dead last to win a progressive Rollover Championship Points Pot.

Points Pot Range (S17) +3 TO +17 PTS Capped at Season #17
Rule 01 • Eligible Challengers

Pole Winner & Last Week's Winner

Offered exclusively to the Previous Week's Race Winner and Tonight's Pole Winner. Both still run normal Qualifying so pole stats count. If one driver sweeps both, the offer stays exclusively with them (does not roll down to P2). Accepted challengers receive a Race Control !eol command on pace laps.

Rule 02 • Progressive Rollover Pot

Starts at +3 Pts • +1 Pt / Week

The Outlaw Points Pot opens Race 1 at +3 Bonus Points. Every week that goes by where neither eligible driver attempts the challenge, the pot increases by +1 Point, up to a maximum cap equal to the Season Number (17 Points in Season 17). Winning from the back claims the entire pot and resets it to +3 Points next race.

Rule 03 • No Participation Trophies

P1 Win or Nothing • Pot Freeze

Zero consolation prizes. Finishing 2nd, 3rd, or Top 5 from the back pays nothing—only an outright P1 Race Win cashes the points pot. If an eligible driver accepts the challenge (takes the !eol) and fails to win, the pot freezes and does not grow that week, carrying over at the same value to the next race.

Rule 04 • Dominance Equalizer

Mandatory 3-Peat Challenge

If any driver wins 3 consecutive races in a row, they are automatically placed under a Mandatory Back-Row Outlaw Challenge for their 4th race. They still qualify normally, take the mandatory !eol to start at the tail of the field, and earn the full active Points Pot if they pull off the 4-peat from the back!
