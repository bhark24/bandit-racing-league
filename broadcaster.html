🟢 STAGE 1 (15 LAPS)
Laps 1 – 15

Opening-lap survival into Turn 1, The Keyhole (Turn 2 hairpin), and heavy downhill braking at the end of the backstretch into Turn 4 / Madness. Watch who stays out at Lap 15 for Championship Stage Points vs. pits early to flip track position!

🟡 STAGE 2 (15 LAPS)
Laps 16 – 30

70% FUEL CAP & TRACK POSITION FLIP: Because tires do not wear out here tonight, pit strategy is all about the 70% fuel tank limit and fuel-only splashes! Watch who short-pits before Lap 30 to inherit the front row for the final restart.

🏁 FINAL STAGE (20 LAPS)
Laps 31 – 50

20-lap shootout to the checkered flag! Passing is tough through Thunder Valley & The Carousel—clean exits off Turn 2 and Turn 13 and avoiding off-track penalty slowdowns will decide the trophy.

💡 KEY BOOTH STRATEGY NOTE (70% FUEL CAP • NO TIRE FALLOFF): Tire wear is NOT a factor tonight—the trucks maintain grip all run long. Instead, the race hinges on the 70% Fuel Cap, track position at the Lap 15 & Lap 30 stage breaks (pitting for fuel vs. staying out for crucial Stage Points), and wheel-to-wheel braking into Turn 2 (The Keyhole) and Turn 4 (end of the backstretch)!
