Bandit Mobile App 50 at Mid-Ohio (50 Laps • Stages: Lap 15 & Lap 30)

🏁 OFFICIAL RACE SPECS & STAGE FORMAT (NOV 4, 2026) 🔴 Open Live YouTube Stream →
Event Title Bandit Mobile App 50
Track & Layout Mid-Ohio (2.258 Mi • 13 Turns)
Total Distance 50 Laps (112.9 Mi)
Stage Breaks Lap 15 & Lap 30
Tire Allocation 2 Sets (Start + 1 Pit)
Fuel Cap & Times 100% Fuel • Q: 8:50 / Green: 9:00
🟢 STAGE 1 (15 LAPS)
Laps 1 – 15

Cold-tire survival into Turn 1, The Keyhole (Turn 2 hairpin), and heavy braking at the end of the backstretch into Turn 4 / Madness. Watch who pits just before/at Lap 15 vs. stays out!

🟡 STAGE 2 (15 LAPS)
Laps 16 – 30

THE TIRE DILEMMA: Teams only have 1 spare set of tires in the pit box! Pitting at Lap 15 means running 35 laps to the finish on that set; staying out means waiting until Lap 30 for fresh rubber!

🏁 FINAL STAGE (20 LAPS)
Laps 31 – 50

20-lap shootout to the checkered flag! Watch drivers who waited until Lap 30 charge on 15-lap fresher tires vs. track-position leaders trying to defend through Thunder Valley & The Carousel.

💡 KEY BOOTH STRATEGY TALKING POINT: Because drivers only get 2 total sets of tires (1 on the truck at the start + 1 in the pits) across 50 laps with stages at Lap 15 and Lap 30, any driver who pits for tires at Stage 1 (Lap 15) has NO fresh tires left for Stage 2 (Lap 30) or late-race cautions!
