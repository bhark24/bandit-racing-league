Season 16 Chase Round 3 • The Bandit Mobile App 50 at Mid-Ohio • Official Race Report

Road Course Masterclass at Mid-Ohio: Benjamin Lacy (#7) Wins Pole, Stage 2 & Leads 47 of 50 Laps to Conquer the Bandit Mobile App 50!

Silencing every memory of his 20th-place finish in Season 12, Championship leader Benjamin I. Lacy (#7 | GFR Racing RAM) puts on a 50-lap clinic in Lexington—scoring a massive 58-point night while Ethan Sikorski (#24, P2) and Stage 1 winner Scott Sanderson (#93, P3, 54 pts) lock down the podium!

🏆 VICTORY LANE AT MID-OHIO: Benjamin I. Lacy (#7 | GFR Racing "Filming the Wild / Reel Earth Films" RAM) celebrates his league-leading 5th victory of Season 16 after sweeping the Pole (1:26.0337), Fastest Lap (1:26.3345), Stage 2, and 47 of 50 laps led in the Bandit Mobile App 50 at Mid-Ohio Sports Car Course!

LEXINGTON, OH — Entering Round 3 of the Season 16 Chase for the Championship—the Bandit Mobile App 50 at Mid-Ohio Sports Car Course—every scouting report circled the 2.258-mile, 13-turn road course as the one historical question mark on Championship leader Benjamin I. Lacy's (#7 | GFR Racing) resume. In the Bandit Racing League's only previous visit to Mid-Ohio back in Season 12, Lacy had qualified 23rd and finished 20th.

On Wednesday night in Lexington, Lacy erased that stat forever. Piloting the #7 "Filming the Wild / Reel Earth Films" GFR Racing RAM, Lacy blistered the 13-turn layout to win the Pole Position (1:26.0337) by more than seven-tenths of a second, set the Fastest Lap of the Race (1:26.3345 on Lap 21), captured the Stage 2 victory, and paced the field for 47 of the 50 laps with just 3x incident points. Crossing the stripe 10.981 seconds ahead of runner-up Ethan Sikorski (#24 | ESR) and Stage 1 winner Scott Sanderson (#93 | GFR Racing), Lacy racked up a near-maximum 58 total points to extend his Championship lead to 20 points with two races remaining!

Stage 1 (Laps 1–15) & Stage 2 (Laps 16–30): Pit Strategy Chess & GFR Control

With a 50-lap distance (112.9 miles), a 70% fuel cap, and stage breaks scheduled at Lap 15 and Lap 30, crew chiefs had to balance track position against maximum stage points. Starting from the pole, Benjamin I. Lacy (#7) immediately checked out from the pack, pacing the first 13 laps of Stage 1 with zero incident points and clocking a 1:26.4508 on Lap 2 before ducking onto pit road late in the stage to set up his track position and fuel sequence—still crossing the line 5th in Stage 1 (+6 stage points).

That opened the door for GFR Racing teammate Scott Sanderson (#93) to take command for the final two laps of the opening segment and capture the Stage 1 Victory (+10 stage points, 0x incidents)! Dylan McDonald (#9 | Legacy Racing) powered home 2nd in Stage 1 (+9 pts) after making 6 quality passes, followed by road-course ringer Connor Gibson (#31 | ZeroFoxtrot, P3, +8 pts, 0x), Kevin Foster (#8 | GFR Racing, P4, +7 pts, 0x), Lacy (#7, P5, +6 pts), Jackson Knaak (#00 | ESR, P6, +5 pts), Michael Rakes (#47 | 937 Racing, P7, +4 pts, 0x), Chad Hartle (#37, P8, +3 pts), Ethan Sikorski (#24, P9, +2 pts, 0x), and Nathan Santos (#21, P10, +1 pt).

In Stage 2 (Laps 16–30), Lacy returned to the point and unleashed the fastest lap of the entire evening—a blistering 1:26.3345 on Lap 21—while leading 14 of the 15 laps to claim his 10th Stage Win of Season 16 (+10 stage points)! Scott Sanderson (#93) led 1 lap and finished 2.147 seconds back in 2nd (+9 stage points, giving Sanderson a race-high 19 stage points on the night!), while Ethan Sikorski (#24) charged to 3rd in Stage 2 (+8 pts) ahead of Connor Gibson (#31, P4, +7 pts — 15 total stage points!), Chad Hartle (#37, P5, +6 pts), Michael Rakes (#47, P6, +5 pts, still 0x!), Jackson Knaak (#00, P7, +4 pts), Kevin Foster (#8, P8, +3 pts), Nathan Santos (#21, P9, +2 pts), and Logan A. Murray (#4 | Roundy Motorsports, P10, +1 pt).

Lap 35 Final-Stage Restart: Three-Wide Hornets' Nest Into Turn 1 & The Keyhole!

The defining flashpoint of the final 20-lap sprint unfolded on the Lap 35 restart following the Stage 2 break. As Benjamin Lacy (#7) got a clean launch down the frontstretch and hit his marks along the green racing line entering Turn 1 and The Keyhole, the entire top 10 fanned out three- and four-wide behind him in a desperate battle for road-course track position!

📸 LAP 35 FRONTSTRETCH HORNETS' NEST: Coming past the pit exit cone on Lap 35, #31 Connor Gibson, #47 Michael Rakes, #37 Chad Hartle, #00 Jackson Knaak, and #24 Ethan Sikorski battle door-to-door in a tight pack while #93 Scott Sanderson (top) fights for room on the outside runoff and #8 Kevin Foster, #21 Nathan Santos, and #9 Dylan McDonald close in!
📸 LAP 35 INTO THE KEYHOLE: #7 Benjamin I. Lacy (foreground) checks out cleanly on the racing line while a 10-truck hornets' nest dives three-wide behind him—with #31 Gibson, #47 Rakes, #00 Knaak, #37 Hartle, #24 Sikorski, and #21 Santos trading paint as #93 Sanderson rallies from the outside grass and tire smoke billows in the background!

As captured in the Lap 35 broadcast cameras, Connor Gibson (#31), Michael Rakes (#47), Chad Hartle (#37), Jackson Knaak (#00), and Ethan Sikorski (#24) went wheel-to-wheel through the left-hander while Scott Sanderson (#93) was squeezed onto the outside grass/runoff and tire smoke billowed at the rear of the pack. Remarkably, not a single full-course caution was needed! Sikorski (#24) threaded the needle cleanly out of the Lap 35 scramble to take over 2nd place and run a personal-best 1:27.0763 on Lap 37, while Sanderson (#93) gathered his GFR Silverado back onto the asphalt with only 2x total incident points all night, ripping off a 1:26.8649 on Lap 37 to reclaim 3rd place on the podium!

Behind the podium trio, Chad Hartle (#37) turned in the Hard Charger drive of the night (+6 spots), carving from 10th on the starting grid to 4th place (42 total points, including 9 stage points) after running his fastest lap on Lap 36 (1:27.9945). GFR Racing's Kevin Foster (#8) delivered another textbook road-course performance—logging just 4x incidents over 50 laps and setting his fastest lap on the penultimate circuit (Lap 49, 1:28.0748) to finish 5th (42 total points) and climb into 4th place in the Championship Chase!

Franchise Team Financials & Mid-Ohio Outlaw Ledger

On the franchise side, GFR Racing dominated the Mid-Ohio balance sheet with 136 team points, banking +$225,000 in race earnings against just -$20,492 in expenses (thanks to super-clean runs by Sanderson 2x, Lacy 3x, Platt 3x, and Foster 4x) to push their league-leading treasury to $3,979,472 (2,724 pts, 12 Wins)! 937 Racing scored 101 team points and unlocked a +$20,184 RPM Graphics sponsor bonus for a +$160,184 gross haul (-$9,848 expenses, new balance $2,665,655), while Ethan Sikorski Racing (ESR) racked up 92 team points behind Sikorski's P2, Santos's P8, and Knaak's P9—unlocking a +$23,144 SimGear Pro sponsor bonus for +$173,144 in weekly earnings (new balance $2,296,928)! Roundy Motorsports also earned an RPM Graphics sponsor bonus (+$10,640) to bank +$80,640 (new balance $2,248,615).
