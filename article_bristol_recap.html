LACY CONQUERS BRISTOL

Benjamin Lacy Takes Win • GFR Racing Dominance • Race Report

Race Update: Benjamin Lacy executed a flawless race strategy, surviving a caution-heavy night to take the checkered flag at Bristol Motor Speedway. Bob Berry finished a strong 2nd, while Wes Fuller completed the podium in 3rd.

The Bandit Racing League went short-track racing for week four of Season 16, visiting the world-famous concrete colosseum of Bristol Motor Speedway. In a wild, caution-filled battle, Benjamin Lacy piloted his GFR Racing Chevrolet Silverado #7 to a stellar victory, capturing his second P1 payout of the season.

Lacy qualified near the front and showed great patience on the short track, leading multiple segments of the race. Under yellow flag conditions pacing the field, GFR Racing kept their strategy clean to hold off late-race charges on restarts. Lacy crossed the finish line as the field sorted itself out behind him, securing GFR Racing's lock on the team standings.

🏁 Victory Lane: Benjamin Lacy and the GFR Racing team celebrate their second win of Season 16.

Platt and Sanderson Maintain Consistency

GFR Racing teammate Johnathon Platt also had a successful evening, finishing high in the points and maintaining his lead in the overall Driver Championship. **Scott Sanderson** had a fantastic run, running clean throughout to finish 6th in standings.

Meanwhile, the runner-up spot belonged to **Bob Berry** of ZeroFoxtrot. Berry put on a spectacular short-track performance to finish 2nd, which also helped catapult Bob Berry to a massive win in the weekly viewer fantasy challenge.

Fuller Completes the Podium

**Wes Fuller** completed the podium in 3rd place after dodging several multi-truck wrecks in the high-banked corners. This strong finish helps Fuller solidify his top-5 position in the overall championship standings.

With Lacy's victory at Bristol, GFR Racing opens up a gap in the Franchise standings, but ZeroFoxtrot remains hot on their heels as the series prepares for Nashville Superspeedway next week!
